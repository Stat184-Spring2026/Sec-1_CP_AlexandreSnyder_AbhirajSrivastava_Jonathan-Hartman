## Data Sources and Acknowledgements

Our data is sourced from The General Social Survey (GSS), which is a project of the independent research organization NORC at the University of Chicago, with principal funding from the National Science Foundation.

We extracted the variables frequency prayer, general happiness, confidence in organized religion, confidence in executive branch of government, confidence in health care systems, and the year these questions were asked using the GSS Data Explorer and Extractor.

We also made use of multiple Open Source R packages, such as tidyverse, ggplot2, etc.
